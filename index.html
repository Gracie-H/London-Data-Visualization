DATA SUMMARY

This dashboard exposes how London’s transport fares unfairly impact different neighborhoods. By comparing local income data (from the ONS) with official TfL fares, it calculates your 'Transit Burden'—showing exactly how much harder outer-zone residents have to pay to commute compared to those in the city center.
