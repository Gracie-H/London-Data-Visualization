URBAN AUDIT: Spatial Disparity

THE COST OF DISTANCE: A Tale of Two Transit Systems Imagine being priced out of the city center, forced to live on the geographic fringe, and then being financially penalized for it every single day. This is the reality of London’s Zonal Matrix (TfL), where those with the longest, most grueling commutes are forced to pay the highest annual fares. This project is a spatial narrative that uncovers Transit Poverty. By contrasting London’s structural penalties against New York City’s flat-fare framework—where an outer-borough worker carries the exact same transit burden as a Manhattan rider—this audit invites you to decode how the very geometry of a subway map can become an invisible mechanism of social stratification.
